**Sensitivity (footnote).** Averaging 2026-07–2026-09, Ramp's published overall index is 55.3%. Reweighting Ramp's three size-bucket rates to the U.S. firm-size distribution (SUSB 2022: small 91.7%, medium 6.5%, large 1.8%) gives 50.6%. Separately, reweighting Ramp's seven sector rates to those sectors' SUSB firm shares gives 46.3%; those seven sectors hold 51% of U.S. employer firms, so this figure describes only them. The same weights applied to Census's rates give 22.7% and 19.1% (published: 22.8%). The two reweightings are not combined: neither corrects for Ramp customers being more tech-forward than other firms of the same size or sector, so neither is a "true" national rate.
